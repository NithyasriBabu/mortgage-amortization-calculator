How the payment is calculated

General baseline method

Monthly rate = annual rate / 12
Payment = P x r x (1 + r)^n / ((1 + r)^n - 1)
Interest = beginning balance x monthly rate
Principal = payment - interest
Ending balance = beginning balance - principal

The monthly rate is derived from the annual rate entered above. The dollar amount of interest changes each payment as the remaining balance decreases. Extra payments are then applied directly to principal.
